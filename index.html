Kilohour

Kilohour is a free web tool that answers a simple camping, van-life and outage question: how many hours will this power station run my gear? Choose a station, list your devices, and it returns a cautious, typical and optimistic runtime, plus a verdict on whether the inverter can handle startup surge.

Specs for 52 models are tied to their sources, and the math (usable capacity, inverter loss, idle draw) is laid out on the methodology page. You can also open the runtime calculator directly.
